# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS — plain static site with no build step, deployable anywhere (Netlify/Vercel/GitHub Pages). Chosen by the user for a single splash page.

## Users

Mixed audience, enthusiasts first: suppressor enthusiasts and early buyers who discover the brand before launch, plus dealers, distributors, and industry contacts checking that the company is real and launching soon. Both arrive with no prior context and need to register the brand in seconds.

## Product Purpose

Night Clerk Cans is a new suppressor (silencer) company, est. 2026. This site is a pre-launch splash page: it exists only to plant the brand, signal that the company is coming, and give interested visitors a way to follow along. Success is a visitor remembering the name and mark, understanding "coming soon" at a glance, and having a social/contact path to stay close until launch. It is not a catalog, store, or spec sheet.

## Positioning

A suppressor brand built around the night-clerk idea: someone is always on duty, and it is always quiet. Tagline: "Quiet Hours, Every Hour." The motel-sign world — crow clerk with room key, roadside sign, lit VACANCY tab — is the brand's own story; neighboring suppressor brands compete on tactical/spec language, not on a staffed, always-quiet motel desk.

## Operating Context

Visitors land from social posts, word of mouth, search, or a shared link, mostly on phones, often before any product exists to show. The page must work as a shareable first impression and as the image that fills a browser tab or link preview cleanly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single splash page: logo, "Coming Soon", and social/contact links. No email capture form (user declined a form for launch).
- No product names, models, calibers, specs, pricing, dates, dealers, or certifications are confirmed — future work must not invent them.
- Social/contact destinations (handles, URLs, email address) are not yet provided; links ship as clearly replaceable placeholders until the user supplies the real ones.
- Static hosting only; no backend, no form handler, no analytics requirement yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Night Clerk Cans. Spelling on the sign: "NightClerk" wordmark with "CANS" beneath; company name written out is Night Clerk Cans.
- Tagline: "Quiet Hours, Every Hour" — part of the supplied logo lockup.
- "EST — 2026" appears in the logo lockup.
- Supplied logo artwork is binding and must not be redrawn: crow clerk with bow tie holding a room key with red diamond fob, standing on a cream motel sign; "NightClerk" script over a red rule, "CANS" in heavy slab beneath; vertical red "VACANCY" tab on the sign's right edge.
- Logo files on hand: `assets/nightclerk-cans-logo-transparent.png` (transparent PNG, primary for web) and `assets/nightclerk-cans-logo.jpg` (logo on mustard ground).

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo lockups supplied by the user (see Brand Commitments for paths). No photography, product renders, testimonials, press, or customer evidence exists yet — none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The mark does the talking: before launch, recognition beats information.
2. Quiet confidence: state little, state it completely; never pad a splash with invented detail.
3. Credible to the trade, inviting to enthusiasts: one page must read as a real company to both.
4. Nothing ships that launch day would have to walk back — no dates, specs, or claims until they are true.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was established beyond the web baseline: the page must remain legible and usable with keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion respect.
